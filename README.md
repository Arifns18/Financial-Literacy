# Financial Literacy

A Flask-based web application designed to make financial education accessible and actionable. The site is split into two main sections: **Theory** (educational content) and **Practicality** (interactive tools and calculators).

---

## Core Features

### Theory

- **Budgeting & Saving** — Budgeting methods (50/30/20 rule, zero-based budgeting), savings strategies, and emergency fund guidance
- **Investing** — Stocks, bonds, ETFs, compound interest, risk tolerance, and portfolio diversification
- **Debt & Credit** — Credit scores, types of debt, interest mechanics, and repayment strategies (avalanche vs snowball)

### Practicality (Calculators & Tools)

- **Budgeting Tools** — Budget planner with income/expense breakdown and savings goal calculator
- **Investment Tools** — Compound interest calculator, ROI estimator, and investment growth visualizer
- **Debt Tools** — Loan repayment calculator, debt payoff timeline comparison, and credit utilization checker

### Additional

- **Glossary** — Searchable financial terms glossary
- **Resources** — Curated external links per topic
